Welcome to Snorkel Park Beach Club at Royal Naval Dockyard. Just an eight-minute walk from the cruise pier, this is the closest beach to the ships. Rent a chair, follow the snorkel trail to the cannon and anchors, or come back Friday night for the bonfire and DJ.

✨ What You'll Find

  • 8-minute walk from cruise pier
  • Chair + umbrella rentals
  • Snorkel trail with cannons
  • Bonfire Fridays 7pm
