Tobacco Bay Beach

St George's hidden gem - snorkeling, beach bar, and calm shallow water

Welcome to Tobacco Bay Beach in St George's, Bermuda. Just a short walk from the historic town of St George's, this sheltered cove is one of the island's best snorkeling spots. The limestone rock formations create calm, shallow water perfect for families. Rent snorkel gear, relax on a lounger, or grab lunch at the beach bar. The famous Unfinished Church is a five-minute walk uphill.

✨ What You'll Find

  • Best snorkeling on the East End
  • Calm, shallow water - family friendly
  • Snorkel + chair rentals on site
  • Beach bar + restaurant
  • Walk from St George's (15 min)
  • Same owner as Snorkel Park Dockyard
